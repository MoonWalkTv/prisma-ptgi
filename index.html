New in v21 · latest v21v2

Materials that feel real. Glass that paints the light.

The two biggest additions since the comparisons above were captured: full support for LabPBR resource packs, and sunlight, moonlight and torchlight that take the color of the stained glass they pass through.

v21v2

Light through stained glass

Turn on Colored glass light and every stained glass window becomes a light filter, like in a church.

  • Colored patches of sun and moon on the floor, also inside the volumetric light shafts.
  • Torchlight is tinted on the other side of a window; path traced GI and skylight are tinted too.
  • Color bleeding: light bouncing off colored blocks tints what's next to them. Clear glass, panes and water stay neutral.
v21

Full LabPBR 1.3 support

A new PBR Materials menu for resource packs that ship normal and specular maps.

  • Real metals: iron, gold, copper and the rest reflect with their own color, with sharp GGX highlights from the sun, moon, block lights and the light in your hand.
  • Parallax occlusion mapping from the height map, with its own shadows toward the sun.
  • Subsurface scattering for leaves, porosity in the rain (porous blocks darken, dense ones turn glossy) and emission straight from the texture.
VANILLAPRISMA PTGI
Ray tracing + path tracing

The sun comes in.
The room lights up.

The world around you is turned into voxels every frame. Rays travel through it to find out what the sun really reaches and where the light comes from.

  • Ray traced shadows block by block near the player, with a shadow map out to 192 blocks for everything else.
  • Path traced global illumination: the patch of sunlight on the floor brightens the walls and ceiling, while interiors stay dark where light can't reach.
  • Doors and windows matter: open a door and the light comes in, close it and the room goes dark.
Reflections

Water that mirrors the real world

Reflections search the voxel world first, then the screen, then the sky and its clouds: islands show up in the water even when they are off screen, and the sun leaves a trail of light on the waves.

VANILLAPRISMA PTGI
Colored volumetric lighting

Every light has its own color.

Block light spreads through a colored 3D volume, block by block like Minecraft's own light, and path tracing adds the bounces. Faces turned toward a light get more of it, faces turned away stay in shadow.

Redstone Lapis lazuli Emerald Soul torch Torch

Redstone, lapis and emerald blocks glow by default. Diamond, gold, amethyst and ores can be switched on in the "Glowing Blocks" menu. A light held in your hand shines with the color of that item, in either hand.

One world, many skies

From cherry groves to snowy peaks.

A hand-tuned palette for the sky, sun and moon, volumetric clouds, and water that takes the color of its biome: turquoise in warm oceans, dark green in swamps.

How to install

  1. Download the zip from the release post on Patreon that matches your plan.
  2. Install Fabric, Sodium and Iris for your Minecraft version (the Iris installer sets up all three).
  3. Open the shaderpacks folder: in Minecraft go to Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs → Open Shader Pack Folder.
  4. Copy the zip into that folder. Do not unzip it.
  5. Select Prisma PTGI in the list and press Apply. Change quality with the Profile button (LOW to EXTREME).

Updating? Iris keeps settings per file name: rename your old PrismaPTGI_v….zip.txt in the shaderpacks folder to the new name to keep them. Tip: press K in game to switch shaders off and on, the same way the comparisons on this page were made.

What changed, version by version.

The latest version is for Prism and Spectrum members, the previous one for Spark. Fix releases keep the same number with a suffix (v20v2, v20v3…), so a new number always means new features.

v21 · PBR materials

LATEST · PRISM & SPECTRUM

v21v2Light through stained glass

  • NEWStained glass colors the light that passes through it (new option Colored glass light): colored patches of sun and moon on the floor like in a church, also inside volumetric light shafts.
  • NEWTorchlight is tinted on the other side of a window; path traced GI and skylight are tinted by the glass too. Clear glass, clear panes and water don't tint.
  • NEWColor bleeding option: light bouncing off colored blocks tints the surfaces next to them.
  • FIXEDFog through glass and water: whatever is behind, above or below the surface gets the fog of its real distance, also when looking out from under water.

v21Full LabPBR 1.3 support

  • NEWPBR Materials menu. Parallax occlusion mapping from the height map with its own shadows toward the sun; it fades out beyond ~32 blocks and never samples neighbouring textures in the atlas.
  • NEWGGX specular highlights from the sun, the moon, block lights and the light in your hand.
  • NEWReal metals (iron, gold, copper…) that reflect with their own color and get no diffuse light.
  • NEWSubsurface scattering for leaves and translucent materials; emission straight from the texture; texture occlusion applied to ambient light only.
  • IMPROVEDPorosity in the rain: porous materials darken, dense ones turn glossy, rough surfaces stay dry (only truly smooth materials reflect).

Technical specs

Under the hood

RequirementsMinecraft Java with Fabric, Sodium and Iris (compute shaders). Tested with Iris 1.11.4 on Minecraft 26.2.
Path tracingHalf resolution, 1 ray per pixel by default (up to 4), infinite bounces by reusing the previous frame, temporal + à-trous denoiser.
Voxel world128 × 96 × 128 blocks around the player, rebuilt every frame from the shadow pass.
ShadowsRay traced near you (64 steps), 2048 shadow map out to 192 blocks. Stairs, slabs and doors cast their real shape.
Colored light3D RGBA volume with two propagation steps per frame, directional light, 18 light source types plus modded light blocks.
SkyPhysical atmosphere (Rayleigh, Mie, ozone) with the Prisma palette, volumetric cumulus, cirrus, stars and the vanilla moon with its phases.
MaterialsLabPBR 1.3: normal and specular maps, parallax occlusion mapping with self-shadowing, GGX highlights, metals, subsurface scattering, porosity, emission.
Stained glassSun, moon, block light, GI and skylight filtered by the color of the glass they cross, also in volumetric light; optional color bleeding from colored blocks.
WaterCrested waves, refraction, per-biome absorption, caustics, voxel + screen space reflections, puddles and ripples in the rain.
ProfilesLOW · MEDIUM · HIGH · ULTRA · EXTREME, plus a lighter mode without path tracing.
